> - - [Airalo](https://airalo.com) - Largest eSIM marketplace. 200+ countries, strong brand, slightly premium pricing. Best for unusual destinations.
>   - - [Holafly](https://holafly.com) - Unlimited data plans. 2-week Europe unlimited ~€29-35. Throttles at high daily usage.

>           - ## Device Compatibility
>          
>           - - [esimdb.ai Device Check](https://esimdb.ai/esim-compatible-devices/) - Search your phone model for eSIM support
>             - - **Quick check:** Dial `*#06#` on your phone. A 32-digit EID confirms eSIM support.
>               - - Most phones made after 2020 support eSIM. Notable exceptions: some budget Android models.
>                
>                 - ## Travel Data Guides
>                
>                 - - [How much data do I need?](https://esimdb.ai) - Light use: 1-3GB/week. Moderate: 3-7GB. Heavy/remote work: 8-15GB+
>                   - - **Hotspot tip:** 60% of eSIM plans don't allow tethering. Always filter for hotspot if you need it.
>                     - - **Regional vs country plans:** For 3+ country trips, regional plans are almost always cheaper (20-35% savings).
>                       - - **"Unlimited" plans:** Always have a fair-use throttle, typically 1-5GB/day at full speed.
